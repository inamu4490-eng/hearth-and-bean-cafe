Our story

A small café, big on warmth.

Hearth & Bean started in 2016 as a three-table espresso cart on Maple Street — today we're a neighborhood spot where regulars know each other by name. Every bean is roasted in-house, every pastry baked before sunrise.

  • Beans roasted weekly, on site
  • Locally sourced milk & produce
  • Compostable cups, zero-waste kitchen
  • Free Wi-Fi & laptop-friendly tables
